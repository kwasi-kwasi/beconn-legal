Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026 · Replaces the version of 20 October 2026

Beconn helps you stay close to people you already know. This policy describes what the app actually collects, why, and who else sees it — not a general-purpose template.

The short version. We collect what the app needs to work and nothing for advertising. We do not sell your data. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Your address book stays on your device: to show who's already on Beconn we check numbers in hashed form and keep nothing, and if you invite someone who isn't on Beconn yet, we keep their number until they join, for up to 90 days. Only people you've connected with see your full profile. You can delete your account and the data we hold about you from inside the app.

2. iOS permissions Beconn requests

Every one of these is optional, requested in context, and revocable in iOS Settings.

Contacts

To build your own list of people and remember details about them. The details of people you import — name, phone number, email, photo, organisation, birthday — are stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework. We do not store your address book. To show which of your contacts already use Beconn, the app sends our server a hash of each number; the server answers with the matches — their name and photo — and keeps nothing.

Beyond that check, contact details are kept on our servers only when you invite someone who isn't on Beconn yet:

  • We store their phone number so the invitation is waiting when they sign up. It is deleted when they join, when you delete your account, or automatically 90 days after your most recent invitation.
  • If you invite them to a beconn, the name you have for them appears on that beconn's guest list, which the other guests can see. Their number does not: the list holds a code made from it with a key only your account and our server hold, so we can recognise them when they join and no one else can work the number out.
  • You send the invitation text yourself, from Messages.

Microphone and speech recognition

For voice notes. Apple's speech recognition turns your voice into text, on your device where it supports it; we never receive or store audio. The text is sent to Anthropic only if you have allowed Smart Notes — see "Smart Notes and AI" in section 4.

Location (When In Use)

Only to tell friends whether you are in town. We store an approximate position, and what friends see is a yes/no — never a precise location, an address, or a history of where you have been.

Calendar

Only if you turn calendar features on. We add your beconns, to-dos and birthdays to your calendar. If you ask it to, Beconn also reads event dates to suggest which days to mark busy in your forecast — never what is on them, and nothing is shared until you confirm it.

Photo library

To let you choose a photo for a beconn or your profile. Beconn only ever receives the photo you pick.

3. Information we do not collect

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not use your data to target ads anywhere else.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. The app never asks for tracking permission because it does no tracking.
  • We do not collect your precise location, or any location history.
  • We do not read your messages, your email, or your calendar event contents.

Smart Notes and AI

Smart Notes is optional. It uses Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, PBC, to turn a note into the people, notes, to-dos and dates in it, which you review before anything is saved. The app explains what is sent and to whom, and asks your permission, before the first note is sent; until you agree, nothing is.

  • What is sent: the text of the note — what you type, or what your voice note is transcribed to. It can include names and anything you say about the people in it. Nothing else: not your name, phone number, account details, contacts, location or audio.
  • How it is collected: you type the note, or dictate it and Apple's speech recognition turns it into text, on your device where it supports it. Beconn never receives or stores audio.
  • How it is used: our server passes the text to Anthropic and returns the result to your device. We do not keep the text; we keep only counts of how many Smart Notes you use, for rate limits and to plan pricing. The text is used for no other purpose.
  • How Anthropic protects it: Anthropic processes it only to provide this service to us, under commercial terms by which it does not use the text to train its models and deletes it within 30 days — except content flagged for breaking Anthropic's usage policies, which it may keep for up to 2 years, or where the law requires. These protections are at least equal to those in this policy.
  • Your choice: Settings → Privacy → Smart Notes turns it off at any time. Once it is off, our server refuses to send any further note text to Anthropic. Without Smart Notes, notes are saved exactly as written, only on your device.

5. Diagnostics and analytics

We record which screens and features are used, and we receive crash reports, so we can fix what is broken and improve what is used. This is tied to an account identifier so we can tell one person's session from another's. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or combined with data from other companies. It contains no message content, no contact details and no precise location.

6. How we use your information

To run the app: to sign you in, connect you with people you choose, deliver beconns and notifications, and keep your account data in sync across your devices. We also use it to keep the service safe — see "Safety and moderation".

7. Sharing your information

Your content is shared only with the people you send it to. There is no public feed and no discovery: nobody sees a beconn unless you invited them. If you invite someone who isn't on Beconn yet to a beconn, its guests see the name you have for them (see "Contacts" in section 2). We share data with the providers in section 4, and we will disclose information if the law requires it.

Your own profile — phone number, email, birthday, home city and the family members you add — is visible only to people you've connected with. Anyone else on Beconn, such as another guest on a beconn, sees only your name and photo.

8. Safety and moderation

Beconn lets you report a comment and block another user from inside the app. Reports are reviewed by us and may result in content or accounts being removed. Reports are private — the person reported is not told who reported them. Blocking is also private: the person blocked is not notified.

9. Data retention and deletion

Your account data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account from Settings → Delete Account in the app. This removes from our servers your profile, the beconns you created and their comments, your comments on other beconns, your connections, your forecast, your location status, the photos you uploaded, and invitations you sent to people not yet on Beconn. It cannot be undone, and it happens immediately — at most within 30 days for routine backups. Your People list, notes and to-dos are stored only on your device; deleting the app removes them.

If you want a copy of your data first, use Settings → Export Data, which writes everything to a file you keep.

Other people's beconns stay, with you removed from their guest lists.

Note that deleting the app is not the same as deleting your account. Uninstalling removes the data held on your device; use Settings → Delete Account first if you want your account removed from our servers too.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Export and deletion are built into the app; for anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

11. Children

Beconn is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

12. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to production systems is limited to people who need it. No system is perfect, and we will notify affected users promptly if something goes wrong.

13. International data transfers

Beconn is operated from the United States, and the providers in section 4 process data there. If you use Beconn from outside the US, your information is transferred to and processed in the US.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

15. Contact

Questions, requests, or anything that looks wrong: beconnappsupport@gmail.com.
